Before You Begin

All New York State DHSES/OFPC and many NYS EMS courses require completion of FEMA Independent Study courses before attending state-level training.


Required Prerequisites

  • ✅ FEMA Student Identification (SID)
  • ✅ IS-100.c – Introduction to the Incident Command System
  • ✅ IS-700.b – National Incident Management System (NIMS)

Complete these prerequisites before registering for New York State training.
