NYS DHSES OFPC Firefighter Pathway

The firefighter development pathway provides members with a structured progression of training opportunities designed to build foundational knowledge, operational skills, and continued professional development.

Basic Exterior Firefighting Operations (BEFO)

BEFO provides the foundation for new firefighters, introducing essential fire service knowledge, equipment, operations, and safety practices.

  • Fire service fundamentals
  • Personal protective equipment
  • Basic fireground operations
  • Emergency scene safety
Explore Resources →

Hazmat for Emergency First Responders (HEMFRO)

HEMFRO provides firefighters with awareness-level hazardous materials knowledge necessary to recognize hazards, protect themselves, and request appropriate resources.

  • Hazard recognition
  • Protective actions
  • Emergency response considerations
Explore Resources →

Firefighter I

Firefighter I expands foundational skills and introduces additional operational responsibilities required for effective fireground performance.

  • Fireground operations
  • Search and rescue fundamentals
  • Fire attack concepts
  • Team operations
Explore Resources →

Firefighter II

Firefighter II builds upon previous training and introduces advanced responsibilities, leadership awareness, and increased operational expectations.

  • Advanced fireground skills
  • Incident operations
  • Expanded responsibilities
